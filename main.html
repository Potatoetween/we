Zambia's Cuisine

Zambian cuisine centers around Nshima, a thick, comforting staple porridge made from ground white maize meal

Eaten mostly at lunch and dinner, it is rolled into a ball by hand and paired with a side dish or stew called a relish

Nshima is the national staple dish of Zambia, made from finely ground white cornmeal called mealie-meal that is cooked into a thick, smooth porridge. It forms the heart of nearly every local lunch and dinner.
